Privacy Policy

relayout for macOS · Last updated: August 2026

relayout is built to do one thing on your Mac and nothing else. It collects no data, connects to no servers, and has no accounts. There is simply nothing to gather.

What we collect

Nothing. relayout has no analytics, no tracking, no telemetry, no advertising identifiers, and no user accounts. We do not see, store, transmit, or sell any information about you or your use of the app.

No network

relayout runs entirely on your device. It makes no internet connections of its own. Nothing you select or convert ever leaves your Mac.

Your text

When you use the shortcut, relayout reads your current text selection, converts it between your enabled keyboard layouts (or changes its case), and pastes the result back. This happens locally and momentarily. relayout does not log, store, or transmit the text you convert. Your previous clipboard contents are restored afterward.

Permissions

relayout requests macOS Accessibility permission for a single purpose: to copy your selection and paste the corrected text back (a simulated Command-C and Command-V), which is the only way an app can act on your selection. It uses this permission for nothing else and accesses no other data on your Mac.

Children

relayout is a general-purpose utility that collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
